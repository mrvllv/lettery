My Dearest,

from Jakarta to Bali

For you, by

I don’t know what name the universe would give to what we have. Maybe friendship. Maybe something deeper. Maybe simply two souls who somehow found each other from different places. You are in Bali, and I am in Jakarta. There is only one hour between our clocks, yet there are still cities, roads, seas, and distance between us. And somehow, even with all of that, you still became someone familiar to me.

I don’t want to make promises that sound too big . I only hope for something simple — that no matter how busy life becomes, no matter how much time passes, we never become strangers. I hope we can always come back to each other, even after quiet days, late replies, difficult weeks, or long silences. I hope our friendship remains something safe, something gentle, something we never have to force.

Maybe soulmate does not always have to mean romance. Maybe sometimes it means finding someone who feels strangely familiar, someone whose presence feels easy, someone you hope life never completely takes away from you.

We have never met yet, and maybe that is what makes this story a little unusual. We only know each other through words, screens, time differences, and the little pieces of our lives that we choose to share. Still, I hope one day distance will no longer be something we talk about. Maybe one day, if God allows it, Jakarta and Bali will no longer be two places between us. Maybe there will be a day when I can finally look at you and say,

“So this is you, outside of the screen.” Until that day comes, I hope we keep choosing this friendship. I hope we keep talking, laughing, listening, and being part of each other’s lives in whatever way feels right. And if God has written a meeting for us somewhere in the future, I hope we reach that day without ever becoming strangers first.

Wherever life takes us, I hope we remain familiar souls.
